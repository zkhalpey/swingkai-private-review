Evidence layer

Scientific, not theatrical.

The product is framed on motor-learning evidence and honest measurement limits.

52RCTs in 2024 golf motor-learning review
PaRPlan · Act · Review over pure repetition
  • External focus and structured practiceBarzyk & Gruber 2024 found external focus, contextual interference and errorless learning superior within their strategy groups. Most studies used simple putting tasks in novices; transfer to advanced players still needs dedicated trials.
  • Plan–Act–ReviewLee & Schmidt argue that practice which over-emphasises endless swings under-trains planning and review. SwingKai operationalises that loop.
  • Face angle priorityInstructional and lab-facing sources treat face angle at impact as the dominant start-line variable. That is an observation priority, not a licence for an automated score.
  • Sensors laterIMU and markerless tools are candidates only after pre-specified error bounds, missingness and reliability tests. No 800 Hz or “proven improvement” claims until measured on device.

Primary sources: Barzyk & Gruber, Front Sports Act Living 2024 (PMID 38419912); Lee & Schmidt, Int J Golf Sci 2014; public putting-mechanics synthesis for face, impact, tempo and launch framing.
